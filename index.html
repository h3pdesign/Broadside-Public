WHAT’S IN THE PAPER

The news you follow.
Set in type.

01 / FRONT PAGE

Every refresh prints an edition.

A lead story with its picture, a rail of second stories, balanced columns and In Brief. The layout adapts from iPhone to iPad, Mac and foldable displays.

02 / SOURCES

Paste a site, get its feed.

Add any RSS or Atom feed, or just a web address and Broadside finds the feed. Bring your subscriptions along with OPML import and export.

03 / SECTIONS

Folders become sections.

Your folders turn into sections, each publication gets its own desk, and Latest, Unread and Saved are one tap away. Stories carried by several outlets appear once, with “Also in”.

04 / PICTURES

Halftone, not thumbnails.

Real dot-screen halftone or black & white pictures. With Plus, add fulltone, duotone, sepia, engraving, colour halftone and full colour.

05 / TYPE

Ten edition styles.

Each style brings its own type, rules and page layout, from Broadsheet and Night Press to Tabloid, Swiss and Magazine. Pick headline and body fonts, five text sizes and an optional paper texture.

06 / NEWSSTAND

More than one newspaper.

Keep a paper for work and one for the weekend, each with its own name, sources and style, side by side on the Newsstand. Save stories for later to Instapaper or Raindrop.io.

A few useful details

Do I need an account?

No. Add any RSS or Atom feed or paste a website address. On first launch you can also tap Add starter feeds to get going straight away.

Can I bring my subscriptions from another reader?

Yes. Import an OPML file in Sources, and export your paper the same way. With Plus you can also connect Feedly, The Old Reader, FreshRSS, Miniflux or Feedbin.

How does X work?

X is optional and part of Plus. It needs your own X developer app Client ID and an X API plan that includes the home timeline.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The last edition is kept on your device and opens instantly, with or without a connection.

When can I download the app?
